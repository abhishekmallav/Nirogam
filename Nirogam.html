Nirogam, the unified healthcare interface

Every health record, in one place.

Nirogam is a unified healthcare interface. It does for health records what UPI did for payments: one simple layer across clinics, labs and pharmacies, with AI that explains it.

Try the prototypeHow it works

Unified record

One consent-based history that follows the patient across facilities.

AI agents

Explain reports in simple language and summarise long histories for doctors.

Open platform

Built on openEHR and FHIR, with RAG and MCP so others can build on it.

Like UPI, for your health history.

UPI did not replace banks. It made them work together. Nirogam does the same for hospitals, labs and pharmacies.

Unified interface

One consent-based record. Doctors see the full history in one place. Patients choose who sees what and can withdraw access.

Explains reports

Turns a blood report into plain language for the patient, with questions to ask the doctor.

Summarises history

Gives doctors a short, readable summary of years of visits, tests and prescriptions.

Answers in context

Doctors can go back and forth with an agent that already has the patient's records loaded.

Open to builders

openEHR and HL7 FHIR standards, RAG and MCP interfaces, and custom AI services for private organisations.

Fewer repeat tests

Every facility sees the same consented record, so fewer tests are repeated and fewer files get lost.
